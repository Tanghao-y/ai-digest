Hermes

A personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Mac and is used by its owner only.

What it does with Google

Hermes asks Google for one permission, read-only access to the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly), so the owner can ask questions about their own mail. It cannot send, change, label or delete mail.

Who can use it

Nobody else. The Google project behind it has a single user, its owner. It is not offered to the public.
